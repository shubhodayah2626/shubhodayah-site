Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what information Shubhodayah ("we", "us") collects when you use shubhodayah.com, our CEI & LPI portal and our services, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details – your name, mobile number, email address and, for orders or site visits, your postal address.
  • Consultation details – details you choose to share for a consultation or report, such as date, time and place of birth, and your concern or question.
  • Sign-in information – if you sign in with Google, we receive only your name and email address from Google. We do not receive your Google password, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Payment information – payments are made through Razorpay or UPI. We receive the payment status and reference number, but we never see or store your card, bank or UPI PIN details.

How we use it

  • To provide the consultations, reports and products you request.
  • To send you order confirmations, payment requests, bills and reports by email.
  • To contact you about your booking or order.
  • To keep your account secure and prevent misuse.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our services (for example, our payment provider, email provider and hosting provider), only as needed for that purpose, or when required by law. If you were referred by one of our Channel Partners, they may see the status of your booking.

Storage and security

Your information is stored on secure servers and is accessible only to authorised members of our team. We keep it only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal and accounting requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to view, correct or delete your personal information at any time by emailing shubhodayah@gmail.com. If you signed in with Google, you can also remove our access from your Google Account settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
